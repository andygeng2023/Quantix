QUANTIX

About

Personal quantitative research terminal

Quantix combines market data, historical analysis, quantitative features, charts and research summaries in one interface.

It does not execute orders.

Data reality

No legitimate provider offers unlimited global sub-three-second market data for free. Quantix therefore uses provider adapters, caching and graceful fallbacks rather than pretending unavailable data exists.

HELP

How Quantix works

Why is a value unavailable?

The selected provider may not cover that symbol, may be rate-limited, or the backend may not have historical data yet.

What does a prediction mean?

It is a model probability or statistical score based on the available dataset. It is uncertain and should not be treated as a guaranteed outcome.

How does live mode work?

The browser connects to the hosted API. Your own computer does not need to operate a server.
